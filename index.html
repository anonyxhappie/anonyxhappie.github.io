Engineering thesis

Make the system
understandable.

I care about software that can be inspected, reasoned about and trusted. The interesting part isn't just making something work — it is making the boundaries, failure modes and trade-offs visible.

01 / EVIDENCE

Prefer observable facts over assumptions, especially when software makes decisions.

02 / SYSTEMS

Design for failure, operational reality and the interfaces between components.

03 / LOCALITY

Keep data and computation close to the user when privacy, latency or control matter.

04 / CURIOSITY

Build experiments to learn, then turn the useful ones into durable systems.
